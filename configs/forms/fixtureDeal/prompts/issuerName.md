# issuerName

The legal name of the entity issuing the deal.

Look for the issuer name in the offer letter or term sheet, typically near the
words "Issuer" or "Borrower". Return the full legal name exactly as printed in
the document, including any suffix such as "Incorporated" or "Ltd" — do not
abbreviate or expand it. If several entities are named, pick the one described
as the issuer. Cite the text items containing the name.
